Recent Projects

Bedford Square

KT Appliance supported Clipper Equity’s landmark Bedford Square redevelopment in Flatbush—the site of the former Art Deco Sears catalog printing plant—transforming it into a modern, amenity-rich multifamily community. The project spans four residential buildings totaling 877 units, including a 354-unit building at 2366 Bedford Avenue. KT Appliance executed a full-spectrum appliance solution—coordinating delivery and installation across all units and shared amenity spaces. Complex sequencing was essential due to the project's phased construction tied to the redevelopment of a landmarked structure.The building's amenities include a café, business lounge, cycling studio, wet and dry saunas, golf simulator, a pet spa, and expansive outdoor green spaces. This project underscores KT Appliance’s capacity to support high-profile, multi-phase urban reshaping projects—from historical site conversion to high-volume residential delivery. Bedford Square enhances KT Appliance’s profile in the NYC market for large-scale, amenity-forward developments.‍

KT Appliance partnered with Quinn Residences and Havenn Homes on Crescent Valley in Gainesville, GA, a 147-home single-family rental community. The scope included supplying and installing full appliance packages in every residence with phased deliveries aligned to lot releases. By working closely with the site team and adjusting to build readiness, KT Appliance ensured smooth delivery and installation across multiple phases, supporting Quinn Residences’ growing portfolio in the Southeast rental housing market
